# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro (static output) with bun as package manager, hosted on GitHub Pages at agency-of-one.dev. The scroll flow is plain TypeScript, CSS and SVG/canvas, with no UI framework. Chosen by Kevin on 2026-11-18.

## Users

In priority order (confirmed by Kevin):

1. Solo entrepreneurs who want a tool, automation or AI setup built for them.
2. Tech teams and CTOs who want a senior engineer or architect to join or unblock their team. They will judge the technical accuracy of what the site shows.
3. Founders with an idea or early product that needs to become real software.
4. SMEs / German Mittelstand. Wanted, but hard to reach; not the audience to optimise for.

## Product Purpose

Agency of One is the brand Kevin Kraus sells his freelance work under: one senior product engineer who covers what an agency team would (design, frontend, backend, cloud, AI). The site is a teaser. It shows, interactively, how much goes into building a product technically, so that the visitor concludes it is cheaper to hire the expertise than to find it all out alone. Success means a visitor writes an email or connects on LinkedIn.

## Positioning

Core message, in Kevin's words: "Products are complex. Skip finding out yourself by leveraging my expertise."

One person has been through the whole cycle (idea, prototype, product in production) many times and covers every layer of it. kevinkrs.com stays the personal CV; this site sells the offer and links to it.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Single page. The visitor scrolls, and the scroll drives one product being built: wireframe, frontend, server, authentication, cloud, ML models processing in the background.
- English only.
- Ends with a call to action: mailto to kevin@agency-of-one.dev, a LinkedIn link, and a link to kevinkrs.com as CV/Vita.
- No contact form, no booking link, no backend.
- Inferred, not yet confirmed: as a German business site it needs an Impressum and a short Datenschutz page, as kevinkrs.com has.

## Product Principles

1. Show the complexity, don't describe it. The flow is the argument.
2. Technically honest: every mechanism shown must be one a CTO would recognise as correct.
3. A teaser, not a brochure: little text, one path, one action at the end.
4. Evidence stays on kevinkrs.com; this site earns the click to it.
